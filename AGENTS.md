# mechkit — project goals

mechkit is a tool for designing and simulating machines and robots.
It supports machine design, robotics, and automation work.

## Goals

- Let users assemble machines and robots from individual parts and reusable
  mechanisms in a 3D workspace.
- Make mechanical connections and the motion they produce easy to explore.
- Support simulation of motion, forces, loads, and required torque.
- Keep the interface concise, simple, and clear.
- Build for macOS first, with a Qt port later.

The project focuses on design and simulation. Real motor control is outside its
scope.

Keep this document focused on broad project goals. Detailed features,
architecture, and implementation choices remain open until agreed with the user.
